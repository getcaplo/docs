# Change or cancel your plan

> Upgrade, downgrade or cancel a plan, add phone calls at once, understand renewals and failed payments, and resume a paused workspace.

Owners and Billing members change the plan on **Plan & billing**. Other members can compare plans but not change them. Enterprise contracts change only through your Caplo contact.

## Choose or change a plan

1. On **Plan & billing**, select **Change plan**, or **Choose a plan** on the Free trial or a paused workspace.
2. On **Choose a plan**, select the plan’s button, such as **Upgrade to Pro**.
3. In the dialog, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** (not offered when switching to a lower plan), check the summary, and confirm.

| Change                                                                     | When it starts                    | What you pay                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Start** a plan, from the Free trial or a paused workspace                | Today, once the fee is paid.      | The full fee for a new period. Any trial credit left stays until it expires.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Upgrade**: a higher plan, monthly to yearly, or more phone calls at once | Today. A new period starts now.   | The new fee less the unused part of the current fee, shown as **Unused … fee**. If the unused part is more than the new fee, the rest is added to your balance as usage credit, which pays usage but not plan fees. The current plan credit ends and the new plan’s credit is added now. Funds you added yourself are kept. |
| **Switch** to a lower plan, or yearly to monthly                           | At the end of the current period. | Nothing now. You keep the current plan and its credit until then; the lower fee is paid at renewal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

To switch the interval of your current plan, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** above the plans and select **Switch to yearly** or **Switch to monthly** on your plan.

Select **Pay … and start**. The dialog says how the fee is paid: with a saved card it is charged to the card; without one it is paid from funds you added, or, if they don’t cover it, on Safepay’s page. With a saved card, **Pay with** also offers your added funds or another card on Safepay’s page. One payment is at most $500, so a larger fee is paid from added funds. A card or Safepay payment changes the plan as soon as it is confirmed, and **Plan & billing** shows the result. If the card is declined, the plan doesn’t change, and the result offers **Pay with another card** and, when they cover the fee, **Pay from added funds**.

### Before a downgrade

The dialog lists what the lower plan changes on the day it starts:

* **Features stop.** For example, call recording stops for new calls and providers set to **Your key** switch to Caplo-managed models and speech, charged at list price. Your keys stay saved and work again on a plan that includes them.
* **History is shortened.** If the lower plan keeps fewer days of conversation history, conversations older than that are erased 7 days after the plan starts. Export what you need first.
* **Limits apply to new things only.** Agents, channels and handoff destinations above the lower plan’s limits keep working, but you can’t add more until you are under the limit.

A pending downgrade shows “Your plan changes to … on …” with **Keep** and the current plan’s name, which undoes it.

## Add phone calls at once

On Pro, select **Add phone calls at once** on the **Plan limits** card and set **Extra phone calls at once**. Each is $15 a month, added to the plan fee ($180 a year on a yearly plan). More calls is an upgrade: a new period starts today, the dialog shows what is due after the unused part of your current fee, and it is paid like any plan fee. Fewer calls take effect at the end of the period.

## Cancel

1. On **Plan & billing**, select **Cancel plan**.
2. Read what happens, then select **Cancel on** with the period’s end date.

You keep the plan and its credit until the end of the period you paid for. Then production conversations pause, and the workspace’s data is erased 60 days later unless you choose a plan. Added funds stay for 12 months. While a renewal payment is due, the paid period has already ended, so **Cancel now** pauses the workspace at once.

Until the period ends, **Plan & billing** shows “Your … plan ends on …” with **Keep** and the plan’s name, which undoes the cancellation.

## Renewals and failed payments

At the end of each period the plan renews: the next fee is charged to your saved card. Without a card, or if the card is declined, it is paid from funds you added. If neither pays it, the payment is due:

* **Plan & billing** shows **Payment due** with “Payment for … failed on …” and the date production will pause. Owners and Billing members are told.
* Everything keeps working for 7 days, but the plan can’t be changed.
* Save a different card, or add funds that cover the fee. A card saved after the failure is charged for the fee once; the plan then renews from today.
* After 7 days unpaid, the workspace pauses.

## Paused workspaces

A paused workspace stops production conversations on every channel. Building agents keeps working, live tests and evaluations run while the balance lasts, and your agents, channels and settings stay. Every member sees why production is paused; Owners and Billing members can resolve it.

| Why it paused                            | How to resume                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Free trial ended                     | Choose a plan.                                                         |
| The plan was cancelled                   | Choose a plan.                                                         |
| A renewal fee was not paid               | Save a different card, add funds that cover the fee, or choose a plan. |
| An Enterprise invoice is 15 days overdue | Pay the invoice; production resumes when Caplo records the transfer.   |

Website visitors see “This assistant isn’t available right now. Please try again later.” Messages on other channels still reach your inbox without an agent reply. Every page of the console shows the pause with **Open plan and billing**.

Paused workspaces are erased

A workspace paused for 60 days is erased. Owners and Billing members are warned 30, 7 and 1 days before, and **Plan & billing** shows the date. Choose a plan before then to keep the workspace.

## Price changes

When a plan’s prices or terms change, new subscribers get them at once. If you already subscribe, Owners and Billing members are told 30 days ahead, by notification and email, and **Plan & billing** shows what changes and the renewal it starts from: the first renewal at least 30 days after the notice. Your current prices apply until then. Changing your plan in the meantime takes the current terms at once.
