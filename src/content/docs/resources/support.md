- **Questions and problems:** email [ask@caplo.si](mailto:ask@caplo.si). Include your workspace name, the agent or channel involved, and what you expected to happen. For the CLI, add the output of `caplo version` and the full command you ran.
- **Talk to Caplo Guide:** the assistant on [caplo.si](https://caplo.si) answers product questions and can book a call with the team.
- **Docs fixes:** every page has an **Edit page** link at the bottom, and you can open an issue in [getcaplo/docs](https://github.com/getcaplo/docs/issues).

Never send API keys, provider keys or secret values by email or in an issue. If you think a key leaked, rotate it first: see [Bring your own key](/providers/bring-your-own-key/) and [Authentication](/cli/authentication/).
