Every workspace is on a plan. The plan sets the monthly fee, the usage credit it adds to your balance, the platform fees for conversations and voice minutes, and what the workspace can do at once and in total. AI models and speech are charged at each provider's list price on every plan, with no markup.

Your plan, balance, payments and documents are on **Plan & billing**, under **Usage & billing** in the console. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.

## Choose how to start

A new workspace starts on the Free trial. The first time you sign in, the console shows **Choose how to start**:

- **Start free trial** starts the 30-day trial with $5 of usage credit. No card is needed.
- **Choose Standard** or **Choose Pro** starts that plan now. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** under **Billing interval** first. The plan starts when its fee is paid; see [Balance and payments](/billing/balance-and-payments/).
- **Talk to us** asks Caplo about Enterprise.

Until someone chooses, the console keeps returning to this page. Only Owners and Billing members can choose; other members see the plans and a note that an Owner or Billing member chooses.

When the trial ends without a plan, production conversations pause until you choose one. Your agents and data stay. See [Paused workspaces](/billing/change-or-cancel/#paused-workspaces).

## Who can see and change billing

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner, Billing | Choose and change the plan, add funds, save a card, set auto-recharge, edit billing details, and see everything below. |
| Admin, Auditor | See the plan, balance, activity, receipts, statements and invoices, and the **Usage** page. |
| Other roles | See plan limits where they apply, for example when publishing an agent. |

Owners and Billing members also get the billing notifications in the console's bell and by email: low balance, balance used up, trial ending (3 days and 1 day before), payment due, workspace paused, data erasure warnings, plan changed, auto-recharge, and Enterprise invoices issued or overdue. Billing emails are on until a member turns them off.
